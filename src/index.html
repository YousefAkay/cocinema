How it works

  1. 1

    Rate a few films

    Score movies you have seen from 1 to 10. Not seen one? Skip it. Quick mode covers 10 genres, Full covers all 15.

  2. 2

    Your taste becomes a map

    Each film's plot was turned into many numbers once, in advance. Your ratings blend them into a taste profile, built in your browser.

  3. 3

    Get picks with reasons

    See your top five, then the rest. Every pick shows which of your ratings drove it, and where to stream it in Canada, the US and the UK.
