How it works

  1. 1

    Rate a few films

    Pick films from every genre or just one, score the ones you have seen from 1 to 10 and skip the rest; Taster asks for 5, Quick 10 and Full 15.

  2. 2

    Your taste becomes a map

    Each plot was turned into many numbers in advance, and your ratings blend them into a taste profile in your browser.

  3. 3

    Get picks with reasons

    Your top five come first, each showing which of your ratings drove it and where to stream it in Canada, the US and the UK.
